X Bookmark Vault

Make something from your bookmarks

Start with your 20 most recent saves. Codex will suggest what you can make and save the one you choose to your Mac.

  1. Open X Bookmarks in your signed-in Chrome profile.
  2. Open or restart the Codex desktop app. Use its @ menu to tag the Chrome bookmarks tab.
  3. Copy the prompt, paste it into that chat, and send it.

Pick a number after Codex shows your choices. Your finished Markdown file will be in the vault’s answers folder.
